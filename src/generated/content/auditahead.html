AuditAhead: AI-Assisted Audit Support for Pharmacy Clawback Defence

AuditAhead is AIM's purpose-built audit support platform for pharmacy clawback defence, built on Microsoft Azure. Through a four-agent architecture, AuditAhead provides AI-assisted, rule-based checks across consent compliance and pharmacy claims analysis - with a required pharmacist sign-off on every claim before any result is finalized. Every batch emits a tamper-evident,independently verifiable evidence record. Today AuditAhead is built for pre-submission clawback defence for Ontario (ODB) pharmacies, with the design extensible to other federal, provincial, and private clawback programs.

Key Benefits of AuditAhead

AI-Assisted, Pharmacist-Reviewed Checks

Four coordinated components run rule-based checks across your audit workflow, with a required pharmacist sign-off on every claim - AI-assisted, not autonomous.

Tamper-Evident, Independently Verifiable Evidence

Every audit action is recorded in an append-only tenant evidence ledger, signed with ECDSA P-384 - a method an outside auditor can verify independently, without relying on AIM.

Minutes Per Batch

AuditAhead processes batches in minutes. Large batches are recommended to run overnight for best performance.

Designed to Support Board and Regulator Review

AuditAhead reviews consent, billing, and the evidence trail, and is designed to produce records that support board and regulator review.

Four Coordinated Components. One Unified Audit Workflow.

AuditAhead's audit workflow is built from four purpose-built components, each handling a distinct and critical layer of the audit process. Checks are rule-based and AI-assisted; a pharmacist reviews and signs off on every result before it is finalized. Together, the components are coordinated by the AuditAhead Master Orchestrator to deliver a complete, consistent, and pharmacist-reviewed audit output every time

Agent

Role

Consent Compliance Analyst (CCA)

Runs rule-based checks on consent documentation, flagging interactions that require pharmacist review for regulatory and compliance adherence.

Pharmacy Claims Analyst (PCA)

Analyzes pharmacy claims data for accuracy, anomalies, and policy adherence - flagging discrepancies that require pharmacist review

Audit Review Component

Reviews consent, billing, and the evidence trail for flagged records, building a structured audit trail for pharmacist sign-off.

Audit Attestation Author (AAA)

Generates the audit attestation document - a structured, signed, tamper-evident output ready for required pharmacist sign-off and regulatory submission.

Built for Trust: Security & Evidence Architecture

AuditAhead is designed from the ground up with auditability and security at its core. Every output it produces is cryptographically signed and immutably recorded - giving your pharmacist reviewers and compliance teams confidence in the integrity of every audit record, and giving outside auditors a way to verify that integrity independently.

ECDSA P-384 Signing

Each audit output is signed using ECDSA P-384, a cryptographic signing method that an outside auditor can verify independently - without relying on AIM to confirm authenticity.

Append-Only Tenant Evidence Ledger (LedgerHead):

Every action and audit event is written to an immutable, append-only ledger - creating a tamper-evident record from input to final attestation.

Required Pharmacist Sign-Off:

Every claim is reviewed and signed off by a pharmacist before the audit result is finalized. Checks are rule-based; AI assists the review, it does not replace it.

Cloud-Native on Microsoft Azure:

AuditAhead is built and runs entirely on Microsoft Azure, scaling securely in the cloud with enterprise-grade uptime and resilience

Reactive Audit Posture Leaves Pharmacies Exposed to Clawbacks

Ontario (ODB) pharmacies face escalating clawbacks, regulator scrutiny, and consent-handling risk. Manual reconciliations between claims, prescriptions, and consent records leave pharmacies exposed between audit cycles. The traditional defensive posture is reactive - assembling the evidence only after the audit notice arrives. AuditAhead replaces that reactive posture with a pharmacist-reviewed, tamper-evident audit record built ahead of submission - today scoped to Ontario ODB, with the design extensible to other federal, provincial, and private clawback programs.
