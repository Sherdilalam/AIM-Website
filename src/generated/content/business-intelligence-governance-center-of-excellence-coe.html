BI Governance Framework

Building Trust, Consistency, and Accountability in Analytics

BI Governance is the backbone of trustworthy analytics, ensuring the right people have access to the right data at the right time—securely and consistently.

Core Governance Components

Data Ownership & Stewardship

  • Clear roles and responsibilities for data integrity and usage
  • Definition of decision rights across the organization
  • Quality metrics and stewardship accountability

Access & Security Controls

  • Role-based access and data cataloging
  • Compliance, confidentiality, and auditability frameworks
  • Integration with identity management systems

Data Quality & Standardization

  • Enterprise-wide KPI definitions and reporting logic
  • Consistent business rules and calculation standards
  • Report rationalization to eliminate redundancy

Metadata & Lineage Management

  • Tracking data origins and transformation paths
  • Semantic modeling and metadata management
  • Impact analysis for changes and dependencies

Platform & Tool Rationalization

  • Consolidation of BI assets across departments
  • Unified reporting standards and shared data models
  • Reduction of shadow IT and overlapping tools

Governance Dashboards & Monitoring

  • CoE performance KPIs and adoption metrics
  • Data quality scorecards and trust indicators
  • Usage analytics driving continuous improvement

Governance Benefits

Our governance framework blends IT discipline with business agility, delivering:
  • Minimized risk and enhanced user trust in insights
  • Sustainable BI use with reduced operational overhead
  • Faster time to insights with governed self-service
  • Compliance readiness and audit transparency
