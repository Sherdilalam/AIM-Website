The challenge

Answers were buried across hundreds of documents.

Critical knowledge lived scattered across a large, growing document library. Finding a single reliable answer meant manually searching through files, often taking 45 minutes or more, with no guarantee the answer found was current or complete.

The team needed to ask questions in plain language and get accurate, trustworthy answers fast, with confidence that every response could be traced to a source rather than invented.

  • Slow, manual search across a large document set
  • No easy way to confirm an answer was current or authoritative
  • Knowledge locked in files rather than accessible on demand
  • Risk of generic AI tools inventing unverifiable answers
Our approach

A retrieval-grounded assistant, built for trust.

Rather than generating from open-ended memory, every answer is retrieved from and anchored to the organization's own indexed documents, with citations on each response so users can verify at a glance.

1

Ingest & index

Documents are automatically ingested, processed, and indexed into a searchable knowledge base, 1,000+ and counting.

2

Ask in plain language

Users ask questions naturally, no query syntax or keyword guessing. The assistant retrieves the most relevant passages.

3

Get a cited answer

Responses are grounded in the source material with mandatory citations, so every answer is verifiable and trustworthy.
