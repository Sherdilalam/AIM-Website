Healthcare Technology Solutions

Technology-powered healthcare consulting and software development that helps providers and payers innovate clinically, optimize operations, improve patient outcomes, and deliver exceptional member and patient experiences.

Leveraging technology to redefine the future of healthcare

The convergence of advanced technical infrastructure, biotechnology, and recent innovations has intensified the need to modernize and digitalize healthcare. This shift is pushing the industry toward solutions that enable collaboration, safeguard data, and leverage emerging technologies, all while delivering personalized, future-ready healthcare experiences.
Our healthtech consulting and software development services help private and public organizations enhance patient care, boost workforce productivity, and succeed in the rapidly evolving healthcare landscape.
Our Healthtech Services

Enhancing Patient Engagement

As patient expectations evolve, delivering meaningful, personalized experiences through digital channels is critical. Our healthtech consultants assist providers and organizations in overcoming challenges and implementing solutions for effective Electronic Health Record (EHR) and Electronic Medical Record (EMR) development. We help deliver tailored experiences across the entire healthcare journey, from patient portals to AI-driven EHR management platforms.

Digital Patient Access Platforms

Designing customized web-based patient portals to establish a unified patient communication framework. These portals integrate with multiple EHR systems and Pharmacy Benefit Management (PBM) platforms, providing a seamless patient experience.

Smart Health Devices

Using emerging technologies, we develop IoT software that supports continuous patient monitoring and delivers real-time data for providers to make informed decisions.

Apps for Caregiver Interaction

Through custom caregiver engagement apps, we assist healthcare providers in scheduling appointments, tracking patient health and medications, and promoting medical adherence.

Custom EMR and EHR Software

We create custom EHR/EMR software solutions that help healthcare organizations boost clinical efficiency and improve patient care quality.

Healthcare Operations Optimization

We develop advanced software for healthcare devices, complying with standards like FDA, HL7, HIPAA, and DICOM. Using an agile methodology, we help organizations transform healthcare services, improve operational efficiency, and optimize their ecosystem.

Healthcare Data Analytics Solutions

By gathering, organizing, and analyzing data from sources such as smart devices, mobile apps, information management systems, CRMs, and other healthcare software, our solutions provide visual insights that improve patient monitoring, optimize workforce management, and support strategic decision-making.

Virtual Healthcare Applications

We develop complete telemedicine software for seamless, contactless communication, treatment, and follow-ups. Our telehealth solutions feature HIPAA-compliant remote and telemedicine apps with real-time video consultations, screen sharing, remote monitoring, and secure medical record storage.

Immersive AR/VR Medical Applications

Our AR and VR healthcare applications enable professionals and students to interact with immersive simulated environments. From VR-based medical training and simulated surgeries to AR apps that recognize facial features, gestures, and objects, these mixed reality solutions open up limitless opportunities in healthcare.

AI and ML-Powered Healthcare Solutions

We develop AI and ML applications for hospital administrators to improve patient experiences, analyze health records, and enhance treatments. Our solutions enable medical professionals to collect data, make real-time predictions, and accelerate disease detection and care.

Secure Healthcare Blockchain Applications

We create blockchain-based healthcare software to securely manage millions of health records on private clouds. This includes financial transactions and data exchanges between healthcare providers and patients, generated by IoT and other smart devices.

Secure Healthcare Solutions

Data lies at the core of healthcare—encompassing sensitive personal information, medical research, and financial records—and protecting it is critical. We leverage our deep knowledge of healthcare ecosystems, workflows, regulations, and stakeholders, combined with proactive cybersecurity expertise, to help healthcare organizations enhance cyber resilience and maintain security.

Protected Cloud Migration Solutions

By focusing on security during healthcare cloud migration, we guarantee data protection and regulatory compliance for a confident transition.

Identity Management and Data Privacy

Simplifying identity and access management across multiple systems while safeguarding patient and member data throughout the organization.

Connected Medical Device Security

We manage and reduce risks throughout the lifecycle of medical technology devices, from procurement to decommissioning. Our security experts ensure reliable detection, defense, and recovery against advanced cyber threats.

Healthcare Interoperability Solutions

The increasing demands in healthcare have driven the creation of numerous medical software systems, which often function independently, causing manual management and inefficiencies. Integration and interoperability solutions ensure secure, timely access and seamless use of electronic health data across multiple systems, improving outcomes for patients, healthcare professionals, and organizations.

Digital Health Strategy & Development

We perform a thorough interoperability maturity assessment, identify and prioritize use cases and capabilities, and create an execution roadmap to guide digital transformation.

API Integration & Management

Enabling smooth integration and interoperability across multiple healthcare software systems by supporting RESTful FHIR APIs, app registration, authorization and authentication, API throttling, endpoint security, and bulk data import and export.

Healthcare Regulatory Solutions

We help healthcare organizations comply with CMS, NHS, FDA, HL7, HIPAA, and other relevant regulations and security standards, ensuring adherence to the latest healthcare requirements.
