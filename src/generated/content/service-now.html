ServiceNow services FAQs

Who is a Certified ServiceNow Partner?

A Certified ServiceNow Partner is a company that has met the partner requirements set by ServiceNow and can effectively implement, customize, and support ServiceNow’s cloud solutions. Certified partners possess specialized training, tools, and resources to deliver high-quality solutions for automating IT services, operations, and workflows. ServiceNow trusts these partners to provide consistent results, ensuring client success with platform adoption and integration. Certification also signifies that the partner adheres to best practices, complies with ServiceNow standards, and stays updated on platform innovations.
