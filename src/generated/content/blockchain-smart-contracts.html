    From Paper Trails to Verified Digital Records

      The Situation

      A Canadian logistics consortium struggled with document verification across multiple parties. Bills of lading, customs declarations, and inspection certificates passed through dozens of hands with no reliable way to verify authenticity. Disputes took weeks to resolve, and fraud was an ongoing concern.

      The Solution

      We implemented a permissioned Hyperledger network connecting shippers, carriers, customs brokers, and port authorities. Each document is hashed and recorded at creation, with subsequent verifications timestamped by each handler.

      Smart contracts automate release conditions when all required verifications are complete. Disputes that took weeks now resolve in hours because the complete verification history is immutable and instantly accessible.
