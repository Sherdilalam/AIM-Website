What is ITSM?

ITSM is the discipline of designing, delivering, supporting, and optimizing IT services to meet business demands. It spans core practices such as incident, problem, change, request, knowledge, and configuration management, each essential to maintaining high service quality and operational agility. In a world shaped by digital transformation, cloud adoption, and hybrid work, ITSM has become a strategic enabler of business performance, not a back-office function.
