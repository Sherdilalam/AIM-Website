Modernizing Your SAP Journey

Technology is only powerful when it's connected.

At Architecture in Motion, we specialize in taking the complexity out of enterprise software. For many businesses, their SAP systems are the heart of the company, but those systems can become slow or disconnected over time.

We are the experts who bridge the gap between where you are, running SAP ECC, and where you need to be, thriving in the Cloud.

Our Core Services

Seamless System Integration

In a modern business, your software should not live on isolated islands. We specialize in Integration, which is the art of making different programs talk to each other.

  • The Goal: When you update a customer's address in one app, it should update everywhere.
  • The Result: We connect your SAP core to your other business tools so that data flows automatically, eliminating the need for manual data entry.

SAP Migration: Moving to the Future

If you are currently using SAP ECC, you likely know that a move to S/4HANA is in your future. We make that transition smooth and risk-free.

  • S/4HANA Public Cloud: Ideal for companies looking for a standardized, "hands-off" experience where SAP manages the heavy lifting.
  • S/4HANA Private Cloud: For businesses that need high levels of customization and specific control over their environment.
  • The Process: We handle the Migration of your data and processes, ensuring that nothing is lost and your team is ready to hit the ground running on day one.

Innovation with SAP BTP

Think of the Business Technology Platform (BTP) as a specialized toolbox. We use it to build custom features for your business without "messing up" your main SAP system.

  • Custom Solutions: We build apps that fit your specific way of working.
  • Clean Core: By building on BTP, we keep your main system "clean," making future updates much faster and cheaper.

Dedicated Support & Maintenance

Moving to the cloud is just the beginning. We provide ongoing support to ensure your S/4HANA environment, whether Public or Private, stays healthy and fast.

  • Proactive Monitoring: We fix "hiccups" before your employees even notice them.
  • Strategic Advice: As your business grows, we help you decide which new SAP features are worth your investment.
