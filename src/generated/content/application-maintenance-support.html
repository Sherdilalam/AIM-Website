Application Maintenance & Support

Extend the value of your applications with proactive maintenance and responsive support. AIM ensures your systems stay stable, secure, and aligned to evolving business needs through ongoing enhancements and issue resolution.

Keep Your Business Running Smoothly—Today and Tomorrow

At AIM, we deliver end-to-end Application Maintenance & Support (AMS) services that go beyond bug fixing and system upkeep. Our approach ensures your business-critical applications are not only reliable and secure but continuously optimized to evolve with your strategic goals. Whether your systems are built in-house, configured from commercial platforms, or deployed as SaaS solutions, we provide the expertise and structure to keep them performing at their best.

Why Application Maintenance & Support Matters

Modern enterprises run on complex application ecosystems. These systems demand constant care—not just to stay online, but to keep pace with changing technologies, user expectations, and business models.
We help organizations unlock long-term value from their application investments by offering:
  • Consistent Performance and Availability
  • Multi‑tier Incident Management (L1–L3)
  • Feature Enhancements and User Experience Improvements
  • Compliance and Security Patch Management
  • Scalable Support for Hybrid and Cloud-Based Environments
With a managed services framework, we minimize downtime, mitigate risk, and help teams focus on innovation instead of firefighting.

Our AMS Capabilities

Full-Lifecycle Support

We cover all four pillars of AMS:

  • Corrective Maintenance – Fixing bugs and resolving defects post-deployment.
  • Adaptive Maintenance – Ensuring compatibility with evolving platforms, APIs, and infrastructure.
  • Perfective Maintenance – Improving performance, usability, and functionality based on user feedback.
  • Preventive Maintenance – Monitoring systems to detect and resolve issues before they impact operations.

Tailored Operating Models

We offer flexible engagement models to suit enterprise needs—whether you require dedicated support teams, SLA-based outsourcing, or shared-service models with elastic resource allocation.

Cloud & SaaS-Aware Services

Our teams are equipped to manage cloud-native and hybrid environments, as well as SaaS application support. We understand the nuances of continuous delivery, consumption-based licensing, and integration-heavy ecosystems.
