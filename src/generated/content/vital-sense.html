Vital Sense: The Safety Net Across Every Window That Matters

Vital Sense is AIM's AI-assisted clinical triage and risk monitoring platform. Five coordinated AI agents fuse vital signs, symptoms, and clinical history into a single risk verdict in seconds - with no action reaching the patient until a clinician approves it. Clinical rules always override the AI. Every decision is traceable. Vital Sense supports maternal hypertension as its first deployed use case, with a platform architecture designed to extend across populations.

Key Benefits of Vital Sense

A Platform, Not a Single Use Case

Maternal hypertension is Vital Sense's first usecase - the same architecture is built to extend to elder care, pediatric care, and any population that needs continuous risk monitoring.

Verdict in Seconds, Clinician-Approved

Five agents converge on a risk verdict in seconds. Nothing reaches the patient until a clinician says so.

Clinical Standards Always Win

When the AI and the clinical standard disagree, the clinical standard wins. Every time, without exception.

Closing the Postpartum Blind Spot

Risk doesn't wait for the next appointment. Vital Sense monitors continuously - so the gaps between visits stop being blind spots.

Five AI Agents. One Clinician-Approved Verdict.

Three agents run in parallel, blind to each other. One fuses their outputs and applies the clinical override. One acts - only after a clinician approves. That is the Vital Sense architecture.

Operationally Safe, Compliant, and Secure

Vital Sense is built for clinical environments where the cost of getting it wrong is a human life. Safety, auditability, and clinician control are not features - they are the foundation.

Human-in-the-Loop Validation

No patient-facing action fires without explicit clinician approval.

Clear Audit Trails

A full, traceable record of what the AI did, why, and on which data sources - end to end, including a decision journal of agent reasoning.

Seeking PHIPA, PIPEDA & HIPAA Compliance

Privacy-first by design. Compliance is built into the architecture from day one, not added at the end.

Transparency, Fallback & Governance

When AI confidence is low or systems are unavailable, control returns to the clinician - by design.
