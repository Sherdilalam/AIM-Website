The problem

Knowledge fragmentation is your hidden productivity drain.

Your organization's most valuable knowledge is scattered across SharePoint sites, shared drives, email threads, and the minds of employees who may leave tomorrow. New hires spend weeks learning what experienced colleagues know intuitively. Critical decisions get delayed while teams search for the right document.

The impact is not just wasted time. It is inconsistent answers, duplicated work, and institutional knowledge that walks out the door with every departure.

How it works

Three steps to actionable answers.

1

Upload

Drag and drop documents in any format - PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, CSV, HTML, Markdown. The system processes, chunks, and indexes automatically.

2

Ask

Type your question in natural language. The AI searches across all uploaded documents to find relevant passages and synthesize accurate answers.

3

Act

Every answer includes source citations linking back to specific documents and passages. Verify, share, and make decisions with confidence.
