The challenge

A health-equity ambition needed an execution-ready plan.

A leading Canadian healthcare organization set out to advance a health-equity initiative, but turning that ambition into delivery required a clear, prioritized technology plan grounded in where the organization actually stood today.

Without a shared roadmap and governance, competing priorities and legacy constraints risked slowing progress and blurring accountability.

  • No shared view of the current-state technology landscape
  • Ambition outpacing a prioritized, fundable plan
  • Unclear governance and accountability for delivery
  • Risk of investment without measurable alignment to outcomes
Our approach

From current state to governed execution.

AIM worked alongside leadership to translate the health-equity vision into a pragmatic, phased technology strategy built for delivery, not the shelf.
