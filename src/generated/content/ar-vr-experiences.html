    From Classroom to Confidence: Safety Training Transformed

      The Situaation

      A Canadian energy company faced a training dilemma. Their high-risk work environments required extensive safety training, but classroom instruction wasn't translating to field confidence. New employees were technically certified but hesitant when facing actual hazardous situations. And the logistics of bringing workers to training facilities were increasingly challenging.

      The Solution

      We developed VR training modules that simulate their actual work environments and hazard scenarios. Employees practice emergency response, equipment lockout procedures, and confined space entry in realistic conditions until they demonstrate proficiency.

      The training runs on standalone headsets deployable to any location. New scenarios are added based on incident reports and near-misses. Workers train until confident, not just until the clock runs out.
