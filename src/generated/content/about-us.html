About Architecture in Motion

At Architecture in Motion Inc. (AIM), we design, build, and support digital ecosystems that move organizations forward. From modern application architecture and cloud-native engineering to enterprise-grade support and compliance-readiness, AIM is your trusted partner in digital transformation.

Who We Are

AIM is a multidisciplinary technology consultancy headquartered in Canada, serving clients across North America and beyond. We are strategists, engineers, designers, analysts, and support specialists with a shared mission: to help organizations unlock value through scalable, intelligent, and secure technology solutions.
With our seasoned professionals—spanning full-stack development, mobile, DevOps, cybersecurity, business analysis, and support—we bring the depth and agility to handle enterprise challenges while maintaining the speed and creativity of a startup.

What We Do

Our services are organized around key domains that reflect how modern organizations build and scale:

Digital Products

We create platforms, portals, and applications that are fast, user-centered, and resilient. Our teams excel in Blazor, Angular, React, and Form.io for modern frontends—paired with robust backends powered by .NET, GraphQL, and Azure cloud services.

Cloud & Infrastructure

Whether you're cloud-native or mid-migration, we offer end-to-end support for Azure, AWS, and hybrid infrastructures. From API Management and Kubernetes orchestration to disaster recovery and logging pipelines, we architect for performance, compliance, and cost-efficiency.

Enterprise Support

Our 24/7 managed support offerings span L1–L3 helpdesk, service desk (Jira), IVR, call routing, and incident resolution tailored to the needs of fintechs, healthcare organizations, and public sector clients.

Business Enablement

We empower teams with actionable insights, process automation, and integrated tooling. Our expertise spans business analysis, low-code platforms, machine learning integration, and ITIL-compliant change management.

Security & Compliance

From SOC 2 and CREST readiness to secure identity via Okta and Microsoft Intune, we help our clients meet industry regulations while maintaining operational agility.

How We Work

We combine technical excellence with deep domain understanding. Our agile delivery model adapts to your environment—whether embedding into your teams or delivering turnkey projects end-to-end. Every engagement is underpinned by transparency, governance, and a strong documentation backbone using tools like Confluence, GitHub, and SharePoint.
We’ve built dedicated teams for clients in healthcare, financial services, transportation, and public sectors—ensuring not just delivery, but continuity and knowledge transfer.

Innovation in Motion

We're not just building for today—we're investing in the future:

AI & Machine Learning

From intelligent document processing to predictive analytics, our teams are applying AI to real-world use cases that drive ROI.

Modern Dev Environments

We design secure virtual dev boxes and test sandboxes optimized for remote workforces, Intune integration, and fast onboarding.

Cross-Platform Synergy

Our architectural frameworks support multi-channel applications, with unified APIs and BPM integrations enabling seamless workflows across web and mobile.

Our Culture

AIM was founded on the belief that great technology starts with great people. We invest in talent, mentorship, and continuous learning—building teams that thrive on ownership and innovation. Our internal HR engine hires both local and offshore experts, enabling us to scale efficiently without compromising quality.
We're proud of our diverse, high-performance teams and our commitment to inclusion, collaboration, and long-term client success.

Clear reporting ensures stakeholders are always in the loop, providing updates on identified issues and risk assessments. Our team then collaborates in workshops, aligning on the next steps and addressing any outstanding concerns to keep the project moving forward.

    Early Issue Resolution and Strong Security Foundations.

    In this phase, we focus on gathering crucial information and setting the groundwork for success. Our infrastructure team begins building a pre-production environment if included in the scope. While often reserved for implementation, introducing new technology here allows our designers to test product compatibility, high availability, network protocols, and potential issues early. This proactive approach enables us to identify, escalate, and resolve issues before they become critical.
    Security plays a central role, with expert consultants collaborating with the client’s security team to assess roadmaps, current policies, and control zones. Together, we outline security requirements that safeguard the entire project lifecycle.

    Analyze Today, Secure Tomorrow: Building Future-Ready Systems with Comprehensive Security and Design

    During the analysis phase, we dive deep into the project requirements, performing a thorough risk assessment. This forms the foundation for developing the system architecture document, where we seamlessly blend functional, non-functional, and security requirements with the current state of the system to create a clear vision of the future.
    We collaborate closely with your team through design workshops and refine the architecture with insights from both security and vendor experts. We’ll define the logical security architecture and align it with leading frameworks like SABSA and ISO 27001, ensuring robust compliance. Detailed security test cases will also be created to ensure nothing is overlooked.

    Define the Blueprint: Crafting Designs that Guarantee Quality and Security from the Start.

    At this stage, we begin designing around the use cases outlined in the solution overview. Our senior consultants will develop pseudo-code, services, and canonical documents, as well as transaction/process flows and sequence diagrams, following our proven methodology.
    Our Quality Assurance Track will kick off the development of test cases for Unit, UIT, SIT, and performance testing using an automated framework. These tests are essential for ensuring thorough regression testing throughout the project lifecycle.
    Additionally, Security Assessments commence as soon as the pre-production environment is ready. We provide a comprehensive report to ensure all security requirements are addressed, continuing assessments until complete.

    Empowering Your Team: Seamless Integration, Sustainable Success.

    The development team will begin implementing the defined use cases in collaboration with the client's team, who will provide guidance, perform peer code reviews, and approve key deliverables under the direction of our Lead Architect.

    Change Management is critical for success, as no project is truly complete without ensuring that the delivered infrastructure and application components are effectively managed. If the project introduces new products to the client’s existing ecosystem—enhancing or complementing current capabilities—we place special emphasis on training and mentoring development and operations staff. Extended support is provided to help the client's team transition into their new roles and responsibilities with confidence in the new technologies.

    Empowering Your Success, Every Step of the Way

Our experienced team at AIM is ready to support you beyond the initial go-live phase, ensuring you feel confident in managing your new solution.
