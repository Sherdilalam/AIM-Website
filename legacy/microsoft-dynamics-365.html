Microsoft Dynamics 365 Advisory Services

Get tailored Dynamics 365 solutions to streamline core business functions with minimal cost and effort. AIM offers Microsoft Dynamics 365 consulting services to help you achieve your goals faster.

We simplify the transformation of your business operations with Microsoft Dynamics 365.

Microsoft Dynamics 365 provides AI-powered business tools that enhance CRM (customer relationship management) and ERP (enterprise resource planning) capabilities across multiple business functions. Our consultants design and implement tailored Dynamics 365 solutions to increase operational efficiency and drive revenue growth. With a diverse team experienced in optimizing and integrating data, applications, and workflows, we create a unified, streamlined platform. Supported by global staff, we deliver around-the-clock productivity to help you achieve your goals faster and more cost-effectively.

Our Microsoft Dynamics 365 Services

Strategy Advising

Our consultants collaborate with your organization to develop a customized Microsoft Dynamics 365 strategy that tackles your challenges and supports your goals. We also provide a detailed roadmap showing exactly how to move from your current state to your desired outcomes.

Solution Design

Dynamics 365 provides a wide range of capabilities and modules. Our consultants guide you in selecting the right tools and deliver a customized configuration and architecture design to ensure your solution delivers all the functionality needed to elevate your business operations.

Implementation Services

Our experienced technical team helps implement your Microsoft Dynamics 365 solution with minimal business disruption. Whether you need staff augmentation to support your existing team or full management of your implementation, we offer flexible engagement models to meet your needs. We also provide ongoing support and maintenance to ensure your solution continues to deliver value.

Integration Services

We help integrate your diverse data sources and enterprise applications with Dynamics 365 modules, creating a unified, streamlined solution that ensures no valuable data is left behind.

Business Process Optimization

Our business process optimization consultants go beyond Dynamics 365 to identify opportunities for eliminating inefficiencies, reducing costs, and increasing revenue. We help companies implement sustainable changes that deliver both immediate and long-term benefits.

Migration Consulting

Migrations can be complex and full of unexpected challenges. By leveraging our experts to move your existing CRM and ERP systems to Dynamics 365, we help eliminate obstacles and ensure a seamless transition.
