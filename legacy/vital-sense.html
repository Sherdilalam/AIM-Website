Vital Sense: The Safety Net Across Every Window That Matters

Vital Sense is AIM's AI-assisted clinical triage and risk monitoring platform. Five coordinated AI agents fuse vital signs, symptoms, and clinical history into a single risk verdict in seconds - with no action reaching the patient until a clinician approves it. Clinical rules always override the AI. Every decision is traceable. Vital Sense supports maternal hypertension as its first deployed use case, with a platform architecture designed to extend across populations.

Five AI Agents. One Clinician-Approved Verdict.

Three agents run in parallel, blind to each other. One fuses their outputs and applies the clinical override. One acts - only after a clinician approves. That is the Vital Sense architecture.
