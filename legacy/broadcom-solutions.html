Broadcom Layer7 Solutions

Helping You Build Stronger, Smarter, and More Reliable Infrastructure

Broadcom plays a huge role in powering the technology we all rely on every day, from data centers and cloud platforms to networking, storage, and enterprise software. At AIM, we help organizations make the most of Broadcom’s technologies by designing solutions that are practical, scalable, and built around real business needs.
Whether you’re modernizing your infrastructure, improving network performance, or strengthening your security posture, our team brings hands-on experience across Broadcom’s hardware and software ecosystem to support you end to end.

What We Help You Do

Modernize & Strengthen Your Network

We work with Broadcom's industry-leading networking and connectivity technologies to help you build faster, more reliable, and secure networks.

  • Setting up high-performance Ethernet and routing systems
  • Improving network speed, resilience, and overall capacity
  • Designing secure and scalable architectures for enterprise and cloud workloads

Our goal is simple: keep your business connected and performing at its best.

Optimize Your Data Center & Storage

Broadcom powers many of the world's data centers, and we use that capability to help you:

  • Streamline storage environments
  • Improve data accessibility and reliability
  • Integrate high-performance controllers and connectivity solutions

We make sure your data environment is efficient, protected, and ready for growth.

Leverage Enterprise Software You Can Trust

Broadcom's software suite supports key areas like automation, mainframe operations, governance, and security. We help you:

  • Deploy and configure Broadcom enterprise software
  • Modernize older systems without disruption
  • Improve operational visibility and uptime
  • Strengthen compliance and governance

Our team focuses on practical solutions that reduce complexity and improve everyday operations.

Enhance Security & Identity Management

Security is non-negotiable. Using Broadcom's enterprise security tools, we help organizations:

  • Implement strong identity and access controls
  • Establish clear security policies and governance
  • Reduce risks across hybrid and multi-cloud environments
  • Automate compliance and reporting

We help you build security that's both strong and sustainable.

Support, Licensing & Long-Term Strategy

Broadcom licensing and infrastructure planning can be overwhelming. We simplify everything by helping you with:

  • Licensing strategy and renewals
  • Cost optimization
  • Vendor coordination
  • Continuous technical support and troubleshooting

We stay with you long after deployment, ensuring everything continues to run smoothly.
