Google Cloud Platform

With certified Google Cloud engineers, AIM helps organizations unlock the full potential of Google Cloud Platform.

Why Google Cloud?

Google Cloud Platform delivers unmatched reliability, global-scale infrastructure, and industry-leading AI and analytics capabilities. Whether you're modernizing legacy systems or building cloud-native products, GCP provides the tools to innovate faster and operate smarter.
‍
Our team helps you harness the full potential of GCP — securely, efficiently, and strategically aligned to your business vision.

Our GCP Service Offerings

Cloud Strategy & Consulting

Transforming your business begins with the right cloud roadmap.

We work with your leadership and engineering teams to define a GCP strategy that drives ROI, reduces operational bottlenecks, and enables long-term scalability.

Our focus areas:

  • Cloud readiness assessments
  • Cost–benefit analysis and optimization planning
  • Architecture design aligned to best practices
  • Security, governance, and compliance frameworks

GCP Migration & Modernization

Whether you're moving from on-prem, hybrid, or another cloud provider, we ensure a seamless transition with zero business disruption.

Migration services include:

  • Application & database migration to GCP
  • VM and containerized workload migration
  • Data warehouse modernization (BigQuery)
  • Legacy application refactoring for cloud-native performance

We prioritize risk mitigation, optimal resource mapping, and cost efficiency throughout the migration process.
