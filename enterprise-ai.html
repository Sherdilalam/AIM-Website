The Problem No One Talks About

The Search Tax

Every organization pays a hidden tax. Knowledge workers spend one full day per week — 20% of productive time — searching for information that already exists somewhere inside the organization (McKinsey Global Institute). Documents are scattered across drives, email threads, collaboration platforms, and legacy systems. The information exists. Finding it is the problem. The cost is not just lost time. It is compounding institutional amnesia. Teams re-create work that was already done. Decisions are made without context that was already documented. The organization collectively knows less than the sum of what its people individually know.

Experienced The Security Paradox was at risk.

Security is simultaneously the most cited requirement and the most common reason AI projects stall. Forty-eight percent of AI-mature organizations identify security as the top implementation barrier (Gartner, G00828446). Yet most AI solutions treat security as a phase-two concern. Security reviews surface late, expose architectural gaps that cannot be patched without redesign, and kill momentum.

The AI Production Gap

Most AI initiatives die between the demo and the deployment. Gartner reported in January 2026 that 50% of generative AI projects fail to move from pilot to production. The RAND Corporation places the broader figure at 80% — twice the failure rate of traditional IT projects.
The pattern is predictable. A proof-of-concept demonstrates conversational fluency in a controlled environment. Then reality intervenes: there is no tenant isolation, no access control, no audit trail, no deployment pipeline. The gap between ‘it works in a demo’ and ‘it runs in production’ is not a technology problem. It is a production readiness problem.

What AIM AI Agents Do

Most document AI tools retrieve text chunks and generate responses. AIM AI Agents is architecturally different — it reasons about your question using multiple specialized tools, selecting and sequencing its approach autonomously. This is not keyword search with a chatbot layer. It is agentic intelligence: the system decides how to answer, not just where to look.

Three design principles that make it work

Ask a question in plain language. AIM AI Agents reads across your entire document library — reports, manuals, spreadsheets, presentations — and delivers a direct answer with citations. Every response links back to the specific source document and passage. No citation, no answer.

Instant Answers with Proof

Ask a question in plain language. AIM AI Agents reads across your entire document library — reports, manuals, spreadsheets, presentations — and delivers a direct answer with citations. Every response links back to the specific source document and passage. No citation, no answer. The agent understands both narrative documents and structured data. Ask about a policy, and it finds the relevant clause. Ask about a specification in a spreadsheet, and it retrieves the exact row. Ask a question that spans both, and it synthesizes an answer that draws from each.

Secure by Architecture

The platform was built for enterprise deployment from its first day. Every organization gets its own isolated environment. Your data is invisible to every other tenant — not just access-controlled, but architecturally separated. Enterprise single sign-on integrates with your existing identity provider. Three levels of access control enforce permissions at every layer.

This is not security bolted onto a prototype. It is the foundation.

Connected to Your Systems

Traditional AI knowledge systems require you to manually prepare and upload documents through a structured ingestion process. AIM AI Agents connects directly to where your documents already live — SharePoint libraries, OneDrive folders, project management systems — configured without code, synchronized automatically. When a document changes at the source, the agent knows.

Multiple Ways to Access

Web interface. Voice commands for hands-free access. Embedded in the collaboration tools your teams already use. AIM AI Agents meets people where they work.
