## 2. BRAND VOICE

Four words define the voice: **architecture-first, outcome-driven, vendor-neutral, and Canadian.**

- **Architecture-first** — every engagement begins with architecture: scalable, governable, built to outlast the project. Translate technical complexity into outcomes a C-suite can act on.
- **Outcome-driven** — tie every claim to a measurable result (faster time-to-insight, reduced audit exposure, accelerated delivery). Name the outcome; do not gesture at it.
- **Vendor-neutral** — recommend the right tool for the job. AIM has production fluency across Microsoft, IBM, Anthropic, Botpress and more, and maximizes investments the client already holds.
- **Pragmatic** — realistic roadmaps that deliver value early, reduce risk incrementally, and build the client's own capability alongside the technology.

**Tone:** confident, precise, senior. Speaks to enterprise decision-makers. Never hypey, never buzzword-stuffed. Prefers the specific over the general — if a sentence could apply to any IT firm, cut it or sharpen it.

## 3. WRITING RULES (hard requirements)

- **Canadian English** throughout (e.g. "optimize" is fine; "centre", "modelling" where applicable). CAD for currency, metric where units appear.
- **Never use em dashes.** Use a plain hyphen with spaces, a comma, a period, or parentheses.
- **Banned/marketing-filler words** — do not use: leverage, delve, dive into, unlock (as filler), seamless, robust, comprehensive, cutting-edge, transformative, innovative, game-changer, "it's worth noting", "that being said", "at the end of the day", landscape (as metaphor), journey, empower, streamline, "in today's fast-paced world".
- **No "It's not just X, it's Y" constructions.** No generic openers, no filler closing summaries.
- **Every claim must be substantiable.** No invented testimonials, client names, or statistics. Draft for a human editor, not direct publication.
- **Confidentiality** — client names, deal terms, pricing, and internal financials stay out of public-facing copy unless explicitly approved.
- **Vary sentence length.** Prose over bullets unless the items are genuinely discrete.
- **Illustrative UI numbers are not facts.** Decorative figures inside page mockups (sample dashboards, demo pipelines, placeholder deal values, "342 tests passed", etc.) are visual dressing only. Never restate them as real AIM metrics in body copy.
